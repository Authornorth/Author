Terms of Service

Effective Date: November 12, 2026

1. Engagement and Scope

These Terms govern the relationship between AuthorNorth Group ("we," "us," "our") and the client ("you," "Author"). Each engagement begins with a written proposal outlining the specific scope of services, deliverables, timeline, and investment. Work commences once both parties have agreed to the proposal terms via email confirmation.

2. Payment Terms

We believe in making our services accessible and comfortable for every author. Payment structures are flexible and agreed upon before any work begins. Depending on the scope and duration of your campaign, we offer the following options:

  • Phase-by-phase payments aligned with campaign milestones and deliverables
  • Split payments divided into two or more installments at mutually agreed intervals
  • Full payment upfront for authors who prefer to settle the engagement in one transaction

The specific payment schedule for your engagement will be clearly outlined in your proposal. We are happy to discuss which arrangement works best for your situation. All payment details and schedules are documented via email for full transparency.

3. Intellectual Property and Client Approval

We take your brand and creative vision seriously. Every piece of content, creative asset, marketing material, and public-facing deliverable we produce is presented to you for review and approval before it is published or distributed. This is not a formality. It is a core part of how we work because we believe your voice should never be represented in a way that does not feel authentically yours.

You retain full ownership of all original materials created specifically for your engagement, including copy, graphics, and campaign assets, upon completion of the agreed scope. AuthorNorth Group retains the right to use anonymized campaign results and general methodology descriptions for portfolio and educational purposes unless you request otherwise.

We encourage you to share honest feedback during the approval process. Our goal is to get it right for you, not to rush materials out the door. Revisions and refinements are a natural and welcome part of our workflow.

4. Performance and Results

We bring deep expertise, proven methodologies, and relentless dedication to every campaign we manage. Our strategies are built on industry best practices, real-time data analysis, and years of experience in the publishing landscape. We commit to executing every engagement with the highest level of professional skill and strategic rigor.

Book marketing operates within a dynamic ecosystem influenced by market trends, reader behavior, platform algorithms, competitive activity, and timing. While we deploy every tool and strategy at our disposal to maximize your results, the nature of marketing means that specific outcomes such as exact sales figures, bestseller rankings, or media placements cannot be guaranteed. What we do guarantee is that your campaign will receive the same caliber of strategic execution that major publishing houses invest in their top authors.

5. Termination

We want to earn your continued partnership, not lock you into an arrangement that no longer serves you. Either party may terminate the engagement with seven (7) days written notice via email. Upon termination, you will only be responsible for payment covering work that has been completed and delivered up to the termination date. Any prepaid amounts for work not yet performed will be refunded to you. We believe this is the fair and respectful way to handle any transition.

6. Confidentiality

We treat your manuscript details, sales data, marketing strategies, and personal information with the strictest confidence. We will never share your proprietary information with third parties without your explicit written consent.

7. Force Majeure

We are humans working with humans, and life happens. If circumstances beyond either party's reasonable control, such as serious illness, family emergencies, natural disasters, widespread technology failures, or other unforeseen events, temporarily prevent us from fulfilling our obligations, we will communicate with you immediately and work together to find a fair path forward. Deadlines will be adjusted, and no penalties will apply to either side. We believe in extending grace and flexibility because that is how lasting partnerships are built.

8. Governing Law

These Terms are governed by applicable law. Any disputes will first be addressed through good-faith email communication before any formal proceedings.

Refund Policy

Effective Date: November 12, 2026

Our Commitment to Fairness

At AuthorNorth Group, we believe refund policies should be straightforward and fair to the author. You are investing in your career, and we respect that investment deeply.

Before Work Begins

If you decide to cancel your engagement before any campaign work has commenced, any payments made will be refunded in full. No questions asked.

During Active Campaigns

If you choose to terminate during an active campaign, you will only pay for the work that has been completed and delivered up to your termination date. Any prepaid amounts covering work not yet performed will be refunded to you promptly. We calculate this based on the proportion of deliverables completed versus the total scope outlined in your proposal.

Completed Work

Once a specific deliverable or campaign phase has been fully completed, delivered, and approved by you, that portion of the engagement is considered fulfilled and is not subject to refund. This is standard practice and ensures that the work our team has invested time and expertise into is fairly compensated.

How to Request a Refund

Simply send an email to admin@authornorthgroup.com with your request. We will respond within two business days and process any applicable refunds within fourteen (14) business days. We handle every refund conversation with respect and transparency because we value the relationship even when the engagement ends.
